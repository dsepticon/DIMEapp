# Milestone 4.1 preliminary name screen

Access date: **2026-09-14**. Search scope: exact phrases and close variants against games, entertainment, software, products and companies. This is a search-engine screen, not trademark clearance or a legal opinion. Search snippets often combine unrelated words; only a clearly relevant exact use caused replacement. The final names and stable IDs are in [the mapping matrix](dime-m4-final-mapping.json).

The owner directed real-world minerals, ores and gems. The twenty mapped names are factual material terms, not invented brand claims. [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/mcs2026) and [GIA gemstone descriptions](https://www.gia.edu/garnet-description) were checked for classification. **DIME's existing rarity, extraction and sale values remain game balance**, not claims about real mineral markets or industrial processing. Gem designation in DIME does not imply that an actual mine would use the same tool or workflow.

Exact-phrase searches found no prominent conflicting title for the selected **Cairncoil Reach**, **Lomrek**, **Tessick Station**, **Crew Ring**, **Loam Crescent**, **Mica Slope**, **Avenbolt**, **Far Shards**, **Pairhold Carrier**, **Widebay Carrier**, **Bayrise Carrier**, or **Fissurepath I/II** in this pass. The same pass covered all 43 final zone labels, all 14 vehicle/ship labels, 12 gear/crew labels, and nine refinery-process labels; ordinary uses of component words were common. For example, “Rail Freighter” is descriptive transport language, and “Arc Draw B” appears as wording in a patent index, but neither result established a conflicting game or product identity for the complete DIME label. This negative result is limited by search indexing and close-spelling coverage. All names need a final rights review before publication.

The prior [three-family naming review](dime-m4-naming-review.md) remains a history of rejected creative candidates. Its Family A mineral column has been updated to real-world materials following owner direction. No creator artwork, logo, dialogue or likeness was used in this screen.
