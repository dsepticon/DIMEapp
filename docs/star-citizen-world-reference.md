Refinable materials in DIME are precisely the 16 ORE rows; their refined output is the same named material in a separate refined cargo category. Dolivine, Aphorite, Hadanite and Janalite are non-refinable, saleable raw, and use hand/ROC extraction only where their source weight is positive. Janalite's ROC weight is zero, so ROC cannot spawn it in the current code. Raw gems use hand or ROC holds and may transfer to cargo ships. Ship ore uses Prospector/MOLE mining holds and cargo ships; refined output uses cargo ships. The recovered capacities remain Hand 12, ROC 80, Prospector 3200, MOLE 9600, Nomad 2400 cSCU, with all other ship capacities unchanged in `CAPACITIES`. Initial ownership is one Nomad and one Hand mining tool; other equipment and purchase prices remain in `EQUIPMENT` and `SHIP_PRICES`.

**Conflicts and reachability.** `ORE_NAMES` and `ORES` repeat the same 20 names without a missing or duplicate key; six asteroid pools repeat ore names intentionally, with no duplicate within one pool. The prior First Shift hardcoded a refinery route for Dolivine despite `ORES.Dolivine.gem=true` and `refined=0`; that path was reachable only through special quest code. It credited a fixed 300 aUEC for 4 cSCU, conflicting with the source raw price of 130000 aUEC/SCU (5200 aUEC for 4 cSCU). The corrected new quest sells the four raw cSCU once for 5200 aUEC, then awards 500 aUEC: starting wallet 0 + 5200 + 500 = 5700 aUEC. The earlier version is reconciled once from verified quest and inventory evidence; ambiguous records require support, and no historical sale or reward is replayed. The tutorial's Lyria supply exchange remains a deliberate local quest exception to the general DIME raw-gem sale rule at Area18; its long-term location policy needs owner review. The game's Lyria and Wala generic gem rolls are currently identical; there is no source-backed per-moon distribution to differentiate yet. DIME's Halo ship-ore pool is selected by asteroid type, not a Lyria/Wala table. All ore prices, exact mineral locations and any future moon-specific rarity are marked NEEDS_OWNER_REVIEW rather than rebalanced.

## Verified gameplay roles and workflow

[RSI's Industrial Gameplay Guide](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/20050-Industrial-Gameplay-Guide) describes handheld multi-tool extraction of Aphorite, Dolivine and Hadanite in caves and on surfaces; ROC and ROC-DS ground mining; Prospector solo and MOLE crew ship mining; gathering into capacity-limited holds; L1 refinery work orders with speed/cost/yield choices; and raw ore or refined commodity sale. [RSI's Daily Life guide](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/16444523494423-Daily-Life-in-the-Verse) distinguishes gems sold as-is from ore sent to refineries. [RSI Commodities](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034785754-Commodities) describes hand-gathered gems, Prospector ore bags, cargo grids and legal landing for trade. These official sources establish the gem/refinery correction. Current official material found did not name Janalite; a [community mining reference hosted by RSI](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/community-hub/post/le-petit-live-guide-du-mineur-au-roc-FOOY5lsAl5Gun) identifies Janalite with the three named gems, cross-checking DIME's `gem` flag. This is community evidence, not an official mechanic specification; details beyond the DIME code need owner review.

The DIME flow is hand/ROC gem extraction → hand/ROC hold or cargo transfer → raw gem sale; or ship ore extraction → raw ore sale at its configured buyer **or** refinery work order → refined collection into cargo ship → commodity sale. [The official MOLE Q&A](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/engineering/17403-Q-A-ARGO-MOLE) confirms Prospector/MOLE do not refine aboard ship. The ROC-DS is a current official two-seat ground miner but is not yet a DIME-owned vehicle or action. DIME's Arbor, Hofstede, Impact and Helix heads and Terraphon, Andirr and Dora crew modifiers are source-defined timing/equipment values; no claim is made that their DIME prices or modifiers match current Star Citizen balance.

Area18 is a commercial ArcCorp landing zone connected to its spaceport by transit ([official locations](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008085873-Locations-in-Star-Citizen), [Area18 departure guide](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360047674554-Now-Leaving-Area18)). ASOP retrieval, hangar assignment and elevators are documented in [Getting Started](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025028633-Getting-Started-in-the-Verse); [Hangar FAQs](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/115013325128-Hangar-FAQs) distinguish hangars from ground-vehicle garage exits. [The refinery-deck postmortem](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/17991-Alpha-312-Postmortem) confirms industrial station decks and shops. Lyria and Wala are ArcCorp moons in the [official monthly report](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/16999-Star-Citizen-Monthly-Report-February-2019). Exact surface-outpost vehicle-terminal placement, ARC-L1 interior arrangement, and moon-specific mineral distributions below are **DIME design proposals**, not verified replicas of current game maps.
