2026-09-14. The owner selected Family A with **Avenbolt** replacing Dockrin as its city and directed real-world mineral, ore and gem names. The approved Family A names have explicit stable IDs in the [one-to-one matrix](dime-m4-final-mapping.json); this document also retains earlier, superseded candidates as research history. Destroya Industries remains the approved central employer. A search is preliminary screening, **not trademark or legal clearance**. Search snippets are imperfect and do not establish exclusivity; perform a formal rights review before public use.

## Creator-brand questionnaire

1. Which colors feel right for Destroya Industries, and are any colors to avoid?
2. Do you own a logo or emblem you would want considered, and what uses would you permit?
3. What tone and humor should dispatch, tutorials and community interactions have?
4. Would you like your creator name used in any dialogue? Every attributed line would be reviewed first.
5. Would you ever want a fictional avatar? If so, what boundaries should it follow?
6. Which public community references feel welcoming, and which should stay out of the game?
7. Which accessibility priorities matter most for Panel, Mobile and web play?
8. Are there topics or portrayals you want excluded entirely?

Unanswered questions do not block the original universe's functional design. No private creator research or unapproved asset use is needed.
